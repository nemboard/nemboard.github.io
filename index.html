NemBoard

A free and open-source soundboard for Windows 10 and 11. Play MP3 and WAV files, MyInstants and YouTube Music through VB-CABLE in Discord and other voice apps.

Download for Windows View source

Windows 10 and 11 · x64 · Free and open source

Your sound library, browser sources and audio controls in one place. NemBoard 1.0.0

How it works

Keep your normal microphone and headphones selected in Windows. NemBoard sends only your voice and the sounds you play to VB-CABLE, while your call stays out of the mix.
